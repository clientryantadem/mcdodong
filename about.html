Our Story

MC Dodong began with a simple dream shared by two friends, Dodong and Ondo: to serve delicious, satisfying, and affordable food for everyone.

Inspired by their love for food, friendship, and the joy of sharing a good meal, they dreamed of creating a place where students, workers, and families could enjoy flavorful meals without worrying about spending too much.

With passion, creativity, and determination, Dodong and Ondo turned their dream into MC Dodong—a place where quality, affordability, and happiness come together in every serving.

More than just serving food, MC Dodong believes that everyone deserves a satisfying meal and a reason to smile. Every dish is a reminder that great food does not have to come with a high price.

Guided by our motto, "Good Food, Good Mood! Busog sa Sweldo!", MC Dodong is committed to making every peso count.
